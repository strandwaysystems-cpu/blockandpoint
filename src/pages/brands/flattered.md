Flattered was born in Stockholm in 2013 from a question rather than a material: why are heels uncomfortable? The founders concluded the answer was lazy construction — narrow toe boxes, no real arch support, weight in the wrong place — and built the brand around fixing that first and making it beautiful second.

The result is the most useful heel in our directory. An anatomically supportive footbed, a rounder toe box than most of its competitors, and stacked block heels in the 50–70 mm range where pitch stays manageable. It is the pair you wear to the gallery opening and keep on through dinner.

**Price range:** roughly $180–$350 · **Origin:** Sweden · **Sizing:** true to length, narrow in width · **Resoleable:** no

## What to Know

**Size up half if your feet are wide.** This is the recurring theme in customer feedback and the brand does not hide it. Prefer the round-toe styles over the pointed ones if width is a concern at all.

**Treat it as a four-to-seven-year shoe.** The soles are bonded and not replaceable. That is not a criticism at this price — it is simply the horizon you are buying, and it should inform what you are willing to pay.

**The style range is the hidden advantage.** Mules, ankle boots, kitten heels, block heels, square and pointed toes, in a properly neutral palette. If you want a specific silhouette, Flattered probably makes it — which is more than can be said for the comfort-first competition.
