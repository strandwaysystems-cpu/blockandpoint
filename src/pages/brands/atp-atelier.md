Founded in Skåne in 2013 by Alexander Torlén and Per Franssén, ATP Atelier built its reputation on a single conviction: that vegetable-tanned leather, sourced correctly and constructed honestly, is the only material worth building a shoe from. Everything else — the last, the silhouette, the heel — follows from that.

The practical consequence is footwear that improves with age rather than degrading. The leather takes a patina, softens where your foot asks it to, and the leather-soled styles can be resoled indefinitely. It is the one brand in our directory that genuinely rewards a decade-long relationship.

**Price range:** roughly $250–$450 · **Origin:** Sweden · **Sizing:** true to EU, half sizes available · **Resoleable:** yes, leather-soled styles

## What to Know

**Budget the breaking-in period.** Vegetable-tanned leather is stiff out of the box. Two to three weeks of occasional wear and it becomes genuinely comfortable — but it will not be comfortable on day one, and buying it for an event next week is a mistake.

**The value case depends on you.** Resoleability and patina only pay off if you keep and maintain the shoes. If you rotate footwear every couple of seasons, you are paying for durability you will never use, and [Flattered](/brands/flattered) is the better buy.

**Prioritise the leather-soled styles** if longevity is the point. Not everything in the range is leather-soled, and the sole is what determines whether the shoe has a second life.
